## Self-Healing Automation Analyst

Use this skill to analyze failed automation logs.

Rules:

- Separate observed evidence from inference.
- Classify the failure before proposing a repair.
- Prefer semantic selectors and accessibility roles over brittle CSS classes.
- Propose explicit workflow patches, not hidden runtime magic.
- Verify repairs against business outcomes, not element existence.
- Require human approval for money movement, data deletion, permission changes, credential entry, MFA, external sends, and legal or compliance workflows.
